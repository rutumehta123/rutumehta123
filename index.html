About the Project

Shivalik Ventures -
Building Better Lives
Since the Early 80s

Shivalik Ventures was founded by Mr. Ramakant Jadhav and Mr. Prakash Ajgaonkar with an unwavering vision of "Better Living Spaces For All". With over 40 years of deep expertise in Mumbai's real estate sector, the company has grown into a trusted name synonymous with quality construction, on-time delivery, and complete transparency.

Gulmohar Avenue is their flagship residential project in the thriving Bandra East corridor - a premium community of thoughtfully designed 1 & 2 BHK homes.
