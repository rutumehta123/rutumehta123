How to choose

The cove reads warm and approachable, and suits you if you want clients to feel at ease. The hillside reads precise and crafted, signals detail-oriented UX work, and sits furthest from the sunny-valley look you reviewed. These are code-drawn sketches for direction only. The real site would use painted artwork in the chosen setting.
